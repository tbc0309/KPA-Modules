## 中文

长按 MODE 2 秒切换触摸屏，每次重启默认开启触摸。模块“操作”按钮可恢复触摸并停止监听。不修改手柄映射，MODE 原有功能保留。

已在 KONKR Pocket Advance / BW03_20260828 上验证开机启动及触摸切换。独立安装，不包含在 KPA-Root 预装中。

## English

Hold MODE for 2 seconds to toggle touch. Touch is enabled after every reboot. The module Action button restores touch and stops monitoring. Controller mappings and the original MODE behavior are preserved.

Startup and touch toggling tested on KONKR Pocket Advance / BW03_20260828. Installed independently; not bundled with KPA-Root.
