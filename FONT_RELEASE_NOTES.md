## 中文

首次发布：MYuppy 常规、粗体两档，无损替换系统字体，停用并重启可恢复。PIF 隐藏模块时，相关应用回退到原版字体。

仅在 0828 固件测试，不保证通过 Play Integrity 认证。

## English

Initial release: systemless MYuppy Regular and Bold. Disable and reboot to restore stock fonts. Apps fall back to stock fonts when PIF hides the module.

Tested on firmware 0828 only. Passing Play Integrity is not guaranteed.
